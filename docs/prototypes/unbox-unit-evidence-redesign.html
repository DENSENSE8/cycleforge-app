Anti-patterns · current

Not AI-slop, but wrong job for the surface

The row is a solid Workbench list item. Fail is architectural: unit evidence is a Station processing job stuffed into an accordion body. Meta chips (qty, location, grade, serials, price) compete with the only action that matters: capture the next unit.

Hierarchy

Eye lands on long title + chip soup first; serial field is secondary. Unbox needs the opposite.

IA

One horizontal Serial slot cannot host photos or notes. Multi-qty lines have nowhere to park per-unit evidence.

Affordance

Biggest opportunity

Promote active unit as the work object. Demote line identity to a strip. Put Line / Support / Timeline on tabs.
