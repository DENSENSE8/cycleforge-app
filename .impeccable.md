## Design Context

### Users

Internal operators and warehouse staff across multi-tenant reseller ops: receiving, testing, packing, FBA, inventory,
labels/print queues, support tickets, operations rollups, and Studio graph authors. USAV is the dogfood tenant.

### Brand Personality

**Kinetic Ledger** — utilitarian, direct, calm under load. Fast, exact, low-friction. Legible throughput over ornament.
State is visible at a glance; chrome stays quiet.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Ops density** (Carbon / Stripe Dashboard): dense rows, filters, fact stacks, tables and boards when data is wide.
- **Linear chrome discipline**: calm selection, keyboard/cmd-K, no size-shifting rows, restrained motion.
- **POS / scan floors** (`density: floor`): one focus surface, big pass/fail, station-down first-class.
- **Studio canvas** for definition graphs only.
- Prefer flat rows, dividers, and plain text over decorative card soup. Typed chips and status tones come from SoTs —
  not random badges. Icons structural and paired.

### Design Principles

1. **Facts and state drive chrome** — do not invent a second story in layout.
2. **Region contract first** (Station / Workbench / Monitor / Canvas), then **data shape → primary surface**, then density.
3. Right pane / inspector is an **optional context slot**, not the product shape.
4. Resolve labels, tones, chips, dates, capabilities via house SoTs — views stay dumb.
5. Compose rails, Monitor blocks, and station scan chrome; grow the SoT when wrong (pattern evolution).
6. Optimize for scan/edit speed and correct state recognition — not visual richness or document whitespace.
7. Preserve actions; make secondary actions visually quiet; keep selection ring-only (never size shift).
